A NOTE ON RESPECTFUL COMMUNICATION

Support, With Mutual Respect

Vellum Studio is built, tested, and supported by one person, and every message sent through the Contact form gets a real, personal reply by email. I'm always glad to help you troubleshoot a bug, work through a refund request, or hear honest — even critical — feedback. That kind of communication is exactly what makes these tools better.

That said, I reserve the right to decline support, cancel a license, or refuse further service to anyone who communicates with abuse, threats, or harassment. Frustration is understandable, and I'll always do my best to make things right; disrespect toward myself, however, is not something I'll continue to engage with.

If you're running into a problem, please reach out through the Contact form — I read every email myself and will do right by you.
