About Me

The person behind Vellum Studio.

Hi, I'm the developer behind Vellum Studio

I'm an independent, self-taught developer building practical desktop software — mostly Python apps with clean, custom UIs, wrapped around ffmpeg for anything video or audio related. Vellum Studio started as a way to solve my own problems with editing and capturing content, and grew into a small lineup of tools for creators, gamers, and other solo builders.

Everything here is built, tested, and supported by one person — so if something breaks or you have an idea for a tool, the message actually reaches me.
